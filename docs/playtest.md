# Playtest checklist

Play the game through once in each pathway, and once in the "Export ban" scenario. Aim for about 20 minutes per run. Note anything that feels slow, confusing, unfair or flat. Report with the template at the bottom.

## Before you start

1. Open `index.html` in a desktop browser, then again in a phone-width window (about 390 px).
2. Use "Roll a name" a few times. Note whether the name, founder and backstory feel plausible.

## The first year

- Can you tell what to do first without reading "How it works"?
- Do the four or five actions on the first turn feel like a real choice?
- Does the "Next milestone" card make the next goal clear?
- Do the missions for the year feel reachable?

## Money and time

- Does cash feel tight in years 1 to 3? Too tight, or comfortable?
- Does ending a year feel rewarding? Note the feeling right after the report opens.
- Do you know why your valuation moved? Check the report.

## Compute and hardware

- Can you tell what a rack costs in megawatts without leaving the screen?
- Did you run into the chip allocation or the power limit? Did it feel fair?

## Rivals and deals

- Buy a 5 percent stake. Can you tell how far you are from 49 percent control?
- After a year, does the rival's asking-price band change? Does the change make sense?
- Send one offer. Does the answer feel like a decision or a lottery?
- Does a rival ever react to what you do? Did you notice?

## Decisions and partnerships

- Read three decision cards. Does each option have a visible cost?
- Leave one card unanswered. Is the default applied in a way you can understand?
- Sign a cloud deal. Do you know when it ends?

## Late game

- Reach 2026. Does the game still hold your attention?
- Does the verdict say honestly where you finished, and why?
- Does the top of the leaderboard feel reachable, or hopeless?

## Feel

- Note any animation that felt too slow, too fast or distracting.
- Note any screen that felt cluttered. Which tab?
- Did any change of year feel like a reward? Which one?

## Report template

```
Pathway and scenario:
Device: desktop or phone, browser:
Final verdict and year:
Most fun moment (year and what happened):
Most frustrating moment (year and what happened):
Anything that looked broken (tab, button, text):
Numbers that felt wrong (what, and what you expected):
One change you would make first:
```
